PractTimer

Temporal Practice Control Unit

PractTimer is a smart interval timer designed for focused practice sessions. Set your own thresholds for short and long breaks, and the app tracks your practice time automatically — alerting you when it's time to rest, counting down your break, and getting you back to work. Built for musicians but useful for anyone who practices a skill with intention: artists, athletes, coders, writers. Inspired by the aesthetic of classic 1980s digital instrumentation.
